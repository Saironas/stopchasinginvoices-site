You are the collections department

You do not want to sour a client relationship

Guardrails: what we will and will not do

The scary part of handing this over is a robot upsetting your best client. These are the rules we run by.

Email only

No phone calls, no texts, no letters.

Sent in your name

Reminders are written in your name and go to your own business customers only after you approve them, from an address you authorise. We never contact your customers in our own name about a debt, and we are not a third party showing up out of nowhere.

No access to your books

We work only from the CSV export you choose to send. We have no login to your accounting system and cannot change or pay anything in it. (An optional read-only connection is planned for later; if it launches you will opt in separately.)

Never threatens

No threats, no legal warnings, no shaming, no pressure tactics. Polite and firm only.

Do-not-chase list

Name any client we must never contact. They are skipped, always.

Disputes go to you

If a client says an invoice is wrong, already paid or disputed, we stop chasing that client and pass it to you with the details.

Kill switch

Reply "pause" (or tell us) and all sending stops for everyone or for one client. See the terms for how fast.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a collections agency?

No. We send payment reminders in your name, on your behalf. We never take assignment of your debts, never buy or own receivables, and never contact clients in our own name. We charge a flat monthly fee, not a share of what is collected. We are not legal advice; see our terms.

Will you recover all my overdue invoices?

We cannot promise that. We send timely, polite reminders and bring replies to you. Whether a client pays depends on the client. The free audit gives an estimate based on your own data, not a guarantee.

What if a client replies angry or says they disputed the invoice?

We stop chasing that client and send you the reply and invoice details. You decide what happens next.

What access do you need to our accounting system?

None at the moment. The free audit and the pilot both work from a CSV export that you send us. A read-only connection to QuickBooks or Xero is planned as an optional feature later; it does not exist yet, and we will not ask for it until it does.

Security and privacy: what happens to our data?

We use your data only to run your audit and follow-up. We do not sell it, and we do not use your or your clients' data to train AI models. We delete your CSV and anything derived from it on request, and in any case within the period stated in our privacy policy. The service providers we use are listed there (Zoho Mail (email), Google (meeting links), GitHub (website hosting), Cloudflare (DNS)). We hold no security certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001) at this time. Details are in our privacy policy.

Do you use AI?

We use software, including language models, to draft reminder emails in your tone and keep the service affordable. In the pilot you approve every email. Your data is not used to train models.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Month-to-month. Tell us and we stop.
